Know your skin

Every skin story is different.

These are starting points, not labels. Skin can change with weather, products and life.

☁

Dry

May feel tight or rough. Try gentle cleansing and a moisturiser with humectants and emollients.

◉

Oily

May look shiny through the day. Choose a gentle cleanser and lightweight moisturiser.

◐

Combination

May be oily in some areas and dry in others. Adjust product texture by facial area.

❀

Sensitive

May react easily. Prioritise fragrance-free options and introduce products gradually.

Hydration essentials

A healthy routine can stay simple.

Look for ingredients like glycerin, hyaluronic acid and ceramides when suitable for your skin.

Your everyday ritual

Less guesswork. More consistency.

AM · MORNING

Start your day gently

  1. Cleanse without stripping
  2. Moisturise to support your skin barrier
  3. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen
PM · EVENING

Wind down with care

  1. Remove sunscreen and cleanse gently
  2. Apply a suitable moisturiser
  3. Add treatments slowly, only if appropriate

Patch-test new products. Seek a qualified dermatologist for persistent irritation or medical concerns.

A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Is this a medical skin diagnosis?

No. The assessment offers general cosmetic guidance based on answers you provide. It does not diagnose conditions or analyse photos for medical purposes.

Do I need to upload a face photo?

No. A photo is optional. In this browser-only prototype, images are previewed locally and are not sent to cloud storage.

Are product prices and links live?

No. Catalogue items and prices are illustrative demo data. Check the brand or retailer for current pricing, ingredients and availability.

Will my data be available on another device?

Demo data is stored in this browser's local storage. It does not sync between devices and is not suitable for sensitive production data.
